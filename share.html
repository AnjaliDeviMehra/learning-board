Share Your Learning

Had a great workplace experience? Learned something useful during an internship, co-op, or part-time job? Share it here so other students can benefit from your insight.

Before You Post

A few tips to make your post helpful for others:

  1. Focus on one specific lesson you learned at work.
  2. Explain the situation — what were you doing and why?
  3. Share the outcome — what changed or improved?
  4. Keep it respectful — avoid naming managers or sharing confidential information.
  5. Write as if you are giving advice to a friend who is about to start the same role.
