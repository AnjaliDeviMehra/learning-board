Browse Student Work Experiences

This page lets students explore short lessons and advice shared by other students from internships, co-op placements, part-time jobs, and volunteer work. The posts are organized by skill category so users can quickly find advice that matches what they want to improve.

Experience Categories

  • Communication: Tips for emails, meetings, presentations, and workplace conversations.
  • Tech Skills: Advice about tools, coding, data, troubleshooting, and learning new software.
  • Teamwork: Lessons about working with coworkers, asking for help, and supporting a team.
  • Problem Solving: Examples of how students handled mistakes, pressure, or difficult tasks.

How I Learned to Ask Better Questions at Work

Category: Communication

Shared by: Mohammad Jalili

During my placement, I learned that asking clear questions saves time for everyone. Instead of only saying I was confused, I started explaining what I tried, where I got stuck, and what kind of help I needed. This made it easier for my supervisor and coworkers to guide me.
