About LearningBoard

LearningBoard is a student project built for DIGT 1302 at York University. The idea is simple — students share something they learned at work, and other students can read it and mark it as helpful.

We built this because a lot of useful workplace knowledge never gets shared. You learn something at your internship or part-time job and it just stays with you. This site is a place to put it so others can benefit too.

How it works

  1. Share something you learned at work
  2. Browse posts by category
  3. Mark posts as helpful
