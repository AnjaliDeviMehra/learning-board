About LearningBoard

LearningBoard is a student-built platform where learners share real workplace insights from internships, co-op placements, and part-time jobs. No textbook theory — just honest lessons from the people who lived them.

Our Mission

Starting a new job or internship can feel overwhelming. You might wonder what skills actually matter, how to communicate with a team, or what to expect on your first day. LearningBoard exists to bridge that gap.

We believe the best career advice comes from peers who recently faced the same challenges. By collecting and organizing those experiences, we help students prepare for the workplace with confidence.

What We Offer

  • Browse Experiences — Read posts from students who have worked in different industries and roles.
  • Skills Guide — Explore the four core skill categories: Communication, Tech Skills, Teamwork, and Problem Solving.
  • Share Your Learning — Submit your own workplace story so others can learn from your experience.
  • Success Stories — Read longer, in-depth stories from students who turned workplace challenges into career growth.

Who Is LearningBoard For?

LearningBoard is designed for post-secondary students who are preparing for, currently in, or recently finished a work-integrated learning experience such as an internship, co-op, or part-time job. It is especially useful for:

  • Students about to start their first internship or co-op placement
  • Students looking to develop workplace-ready skills
  • Recent graduates reflecting on what they learned at work
  • Anyone curious about what real entry-level work looks like
