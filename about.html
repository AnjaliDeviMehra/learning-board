About LearningBoard

LearningBoard is a student project built for DIGT 1302 at York University. The idea is simple — students share something they learned at work, and other students can read it and mark it as helpful.

We built this because a lot of useful workplace knowledge never gets shared. You learn something at your internship or part-time job and it just stays with you. This site is a place to put it so others can benefit too.

Our Mission

  • 🎯
    Bridge the gap between school and work

    Textbooks teach theory. Real jobs teach everything else. LearningBoard captures the lessons that only come from actually being there.

  • 🤝
    Make peer knowledge accessible

    The best advice often comes from someone one step ahead of you — not a professor or a manager, but another student who just figured it out.

  • 🌱
    Build a culture of sharing

    Every post on LearningBoard is a student choosing to give back. We want that to become the norm, not the exception.
