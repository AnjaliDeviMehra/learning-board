Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about using LearningBoard. If your question isn't listed here, feel free to contact us.

General

What is LearningBoard?
LearningBoard is a student-driven platform where learners share real workplace insights from internships, co-op placements, and part-time jobs. It helps students prepare for professional environments by learning from their peers' experiences.
Who can use LearningBoard?
Anyone can browse and read posts. The platform is designed primarily for post-secondary students who are preparing for, currently in, or recently finished a work-integrated learning experience.
Is LearningBoard free to use?
Yes. LearningBoard is completely free. It was built as a student project at York University and is hosted on GitHub Pages at no cost.

Sharing Experiences

How do I share my workplace experience?
Go to the Share Your Learning page, fill out the form with details about your role, company, and the lesson you learned, then submit it. Your post will appear on the Browse Experiences page.
Do I need to use my real name?
You can use your first name, a nickname, or initials — whatever you are comfortable with. We do not require a full legal name.
What kind of experiences can I share?
Anything you learned at work that could help another student. This could be a communication tip, a technical skill, a teamwork strategy, or a problem-solving approach. Posts should be respectful and constructive.
Can I edit or delete my post after submitting?
Currently, posts cannot be edited or deleted through the site. If you need a change, please contact us and we will help.

Browsing and Interacting

How do I find posts about a specific skill?
On the Browse Experiences page, you can filter posts by category: Communication, Tech Skills, Teamwork, or Problem Solving. You can also search by keyword.
What does the "Helpful" button do?
Clicking "Helpful" lets the author know that their post was useful to you. Posts with more helpful votes appear higher in the feed, making it easier for others to find valuable content.
Can I save posts to read later?
Yes. Click the "Save" button on any post to bookmark it. You can view your saved posts from the Browse Experiences page using the saved filter.

Technical

What technologies is LearningBoard built with?
LearningBoard uses HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript — no external frameworks or libraries. It is hosted on GitHub Pages.
Does LearningBoard store my data?
LearningBoard is a front-end application and does not have a back-end server or database. Any data entered into forms is handled on the client side only. In the future, local storage may be used to save preferences.
I found a bug. How do I report it?
Please use the Contact page or open an issue on our GitHub repository.

Still Have Questions?

If you didn't find what you were looking for, reach out through our Contact page. We are happy to help.
